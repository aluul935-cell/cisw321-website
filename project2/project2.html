The Vibrant Beauty Of Red Canaries 

The red canary is one of the most stunning and popular domestic bird varieties. Known for their striking coloration ranging from deep orange to vivid red, these birds bring both visual brilliance and wonderful singing to any home.

Unlike standard yellow canaries, the unique red coloration is the result of selective breeding that began by crossing domestic yellow canaries with the red siskin in the mid-20th century.

ORIGINS 

Created through successful hybridization with South American red siskins, establishing a completely new standard in cage bird coloration. 

SONG & TEMPERAMENT 

Possess melodic, cheerful songs similar to other roller canaries while maintaining an active, alert, and generally peaceful disposition. 

LIFESPAN & CARE 

With proper care, a healthy diet, and a spacious flight cage, red canaries typically live between 10 to 15 years in captivity. 

ENVIRONMENT 

Red canaries thrive in well-lit, draft-free indoor environments with plenty of space for flight and proper perches.
